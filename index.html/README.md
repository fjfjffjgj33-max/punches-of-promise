<section class="about" id="about">

<h2>About Us</h2>

<p>
Punches of Promise is a youth boxing initiative focused on supporting students at Bright Achievers High School in Kenya. Through mentorship, training, and community support, we aim to create opportunity, structure, and hope for young people facing difficult circumstances.
</p>

<p>
While our work currently centers around Bright Achievers High School, we hope to build partnerships and expand our impact across Kenya in the future.
</p>

</section>
